# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In practice, for this repository that means:

- Discuss the code, the evidence and the design — not the person.
- Assume good faith, but do not accept unverified claims as facts. Asking "what receipt or test
  backs this?" is expected and is not hostility.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or publishing someone's private data, keys, archives or
  telemetry in issues.
- Security disagreements are resolved privately first, through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Enforcement is by the maintainer: comments may be edited or removed, and repeated violations lead
to being blocked from the repository. To report a problem, email **support@inceptless.com**;
reports are handled confidentially.
