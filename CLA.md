# Inceptless Contributor License Agreement, version 1.0 (DRAFT)

> **Status: draft for legal review, 2026-11-10.** Not yet in force. It becomes effective only when a lawyer has reviewed it and Inceptless publishes it as version 1.0 in a project repository. The same text applies to every open-source project initiated by Inceptless (Poieweave, Traceweave, Tacitweave, Choroweave, Axioweave, Phainoweave, Koinoweave, Hyphaweave), so contributors sign it once.

Thank you for contributing. This agreement makes clear what rights you give Inceptless in your contributions, so that the projects can stay open source and can also be offered under other terms (for example a commercial licence) by Inceptless. You keep the copyright in your contributions.

## 1. Definitions

- **"You"** means the individual, or the legal entity on whose behalf you sign, that makes a Contribution.
- **"Inceptless"** means Inceptless, the initiator and maintainer of the Projects. Contact: support@inceptless.com.
- **"Project"** means any open-source project initiated and maintained by Inceptless whose repository refers to this agreement.
- **"Contribution"** means any work of authorship, including code, documentation, specifications, schemas, test data, and artwork, that You intentionally submit to a Project, for example by pull request, patch, issue attachment, or email, from the time You sign this agreement. It does not include anything You clearly mark in writing as "Not a Contribution".

## 2. Copyright licence

You grant Inceptless, and recipients of software distributed by Inceptless, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright licence to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense, and distribute Your Contributions and derivative works of them, **under any licence terms, including open-source and proprietary terms.**

## 3. Patent licence

You grant Inceptless, and recipients of software distributed by Inceptless, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable (except as stated in this section) patent licence to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and otherwise transfer the Project, where the licence applies only to patent claims You can license that are necessarily infringed by Your Contribution alone or by its combination with the Project to which it was submitted. If any entity starts patent litigation alleging that a Contribution or the Project constitutes patent infringement, any patent licences granted to that entity under this agreement for that Contribution or Project end on the date the litigation is filed.

## 4. Inceptless's commitment to you

In return, Inceptless commits that every Contribution it accepts will **also remain available under the open-source licence that applied to that part of the Project when the Contribution was accepted**, or under a later version of that licence, or under another licence approved by the Open Source Initiative. Inceptless may additionally offer the Project under other terms, but will not withdraw the open-source availability of accepted Contributions.

## 5. Your representations

You represent that:

1. each Contribution is Your original creation, or You have the right to submit it under this agreement, and You identify any third-party material and its licence in the submission;
2. if Your employer or another party has rights in Your Contributions, You have received permission to make them on its behalf, or Your employer has signed this agreement as an entity;
3. to Your knowledge, the Contribution does not violate any third party's rights;
4. if a Contribution contains material generated with the help of an AI model, You have reviewed it, You have the right to submit it, and You state the model and any applicable licence terms in the submission when the Project's contribution guide asks for it.

You will tell Inceptless if any of these representations stops being accurate.

## 6. No obligation, no warranty

Inceptless is not obliged to use any Contribution. Unless required by law or agreed in writing, You provide Contributions "as is", without warranties or conditions of any kind.

## 7. Moral rights

To the extent permitted by applicable law, You waive, or agree not to assert, any moral rights in Your Contributions against Inceptless or recipients of the Project, for uses permitted by this agreement. Attribution is kept in the Project's version history and notices.

## 8. Signing

You sign by accepting this agreement through the CLA signing tool linked from the Project's contribution guide, using Your source-hosting account. An entity signs by sending a signed copy, naming the people authorised to contribute on its behalf, to support@inceptless.com. Inceptless keeps a record of each signature with this agreement's version.

## 9. Versions

Inceptless may publish new versions of this agreement. A new version applies only to Contributions made after You accept it; Contributions made under this version stay under this version.

### Notes for the reviewer (remove before publication)

- **Purpose**: allow dual licensing (open-source copyleft plus a commercial licence) while keeping contributors' copyright, following the shape of the Apache Individual CLA and the Harmony "licence" variant. Section 4 is the outbound promise that protects contributors from the Project being closed.
- **Who receives the rights (most important)**: if Inceptless is not yet a registered legal entity, the grantee must be the founder personally (for example "[full legal name], doing business as Inceptless"), with a clause allowing assignment to a future Inceptless company. The same applies to the "Copyright 2026 Inceptless" lines in the repositories.
- **Open questions**: governing law and venue (not stated); the legal entity name and registration of "Inceptless" (only the domain inceptless.com is registered; no company or trademark registration is assumed); whether an entity CLA needs a separate document; minors as contributors.
