# Traceform Research

> Subproject of Traceform (Inceptless)  
> Unsupervised Latent Manifold Discovery, 128D Continuous Sensorium & Dynamical System Modeling

---

## Overview

Traceform Research is the foundational scientific research subproject under Traceform, dedicated to discovering low-dimensional manifold representations from continuous natural human trajectories without manual supervision or behavioral labels.

Within the broader Traceform ecosystem, the research provides theoretical and mathematical foundations for the continuous sensorium, associative memory representations, and physical context awareness utilized by Traceform OS and Traceform Runtime.

---

## Architectural Modules

```
research/traceform/
├── representation/     128D continuous manifold lattice, data-driven quantization, provenance tracking
├── dynamics/           Continuous drift tracking, bifurcation detection, multiscale temporal dynamics
├── discovery/          Recurrence quantification analysis (RQA), dynamical motifs, state transition graphs
├── forecasting/        Continuous sandbox forward simulation, periodic spectral analysis
├── probes/             Multi-channel digital and physical sensorium feature extraction
├── intervention/       Closed-loop resonance hooks and contextual policy evaluation
├── null_models/        Surrogate trajectory generation for statistical significance testing
├── evolution/          Guarded parameter calibration and autonomous latent space maintenance
├── stability/          Temporal validation and manifold drift detection
└── semantics/          Candidate semantic projection and observable profiling
```

---

## Relationship to Traceform Ecosystem

- **Traceform OS**: Integrates the lightweight physical sensorium manager derived from research models to classify device context (stationary, walking, pocket gating) in real time on mobile hardware.
- **Traceform Runtime**: Leverages embedding projections and cosine similarity lattices developed here for associative memory ranking and consolidation.
- **Traceform Protocol**: Defines state exchange schemas that encode multimodal manifold states for cross-device migration.
