# Camping Preparation Checklist

- Confirm campsite reservation and rules.
- Check weather forecast and pack layers.
- Bring tent, stakes, sleeping bag, pad.
- Pack water, food, cooler, cooking gear.
- Include first-aid kit, flashlight, batteries.
- Bring matches, fire starter, tarp, rope.
- Pack sunscreen, insect repellent, hygiene items.
- Test gear and charge devices.
- Share itinerary with a trusted contact.
- Leave no trace and pack out trash.
